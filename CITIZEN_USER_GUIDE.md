# CivicResolve — Citizen User Guide
## குடிமக்கள் வழிகாட்டி (Tamil Nadu Municipal Grievance Redressal)

> **Notice:** CivicResolve is a municipal grievance redressal pilot platform. Use this platform to file civic complaints, track remediation status, and submit feedback.

---

## 1. Registering an Account (பதிவு செய்தல்)

1. Navigate to the CivicResolve homepage.
2. Click **Login** / **உள்நுழை** in the top navigation bar.
3. Enter your **10-digit Indian Mobile Number** (e.g., `9840123456`).
4. Click **Get Verification Code** / **OTP குறியீடு பெறுக**.
5. An OTP will be simulated in the developer console (or sent to your phone via SMS if a gateway is configured).
6. Enter the 6-digit verification code.
7. If this is your first time, complete your profile:
   - Full Name
   - Email Address
   - District (e.g., Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, Tiruchirappalli)
   - Municipality / Corporation (e.g., Greater Chennai Corporation)
   - Ward (e.g., Ward 114)
8. Click **Save & Continue**.

---

## 2. Returning Citizen Sign-In (மீண்டும் உள்நுழைதல்)

When you return to CivicResolve:
1. Enter your registered mobile number and verify via OTP.
2. You will **immediately** be logged in to your existing profile without re-entering your name, ward, or municipality.
3. All previous grievances linked to your phone number will automatically appear on your dashboard.

---

## 3. Submitting a Civic Grievance (மனு பதிவு செய்தல்)

1. Click **File Complaint** / **மனு பதிவு** on the header or hero banner.
2. Follow the 4-step wizard:
   - **Step 1: Category Selection:** Select the civic issue (Road Damage, Water Supply, Streetlight, Garbage, Sewage, Drainage, Public Health, Encroachment, Stray Animals, Parks).
   - **Step 2: Location Details:**
     - Option A: Click **Detect GPS Location** to automatically pin your precise coordinates.
     - Option B: Drag the map pin to the exact location.
     - Option C: Enter the street address manually.
     - Confirm your District and Ward.
   - **Step 3: Issue Description:** Enter a descriptive title and detailed summary of the public inconvenience. Optionally upload a photograph.
   - **Step 4: Review & Submit:** Review the auto-classified SLA deadline and submit.
3. Upon submission, you will receive a unique **Grievance Reference ID** (e.g., `CR-TN-2026-XXXXXX`). Save this code for tracking.

---

## 4. Tracking Grievance Progress (மனு நிலை கண்காணிப்பு)

1. Click **Track Status** / **நிலை அறிதல்** in the header.
2. Enter your Reference ID (e.g., `CR-TN-2026-1042`).
3. View the live tracking timeline:
   - **Submitted:** Grievance received.
   - **Under Review:** AI classification & safety index processed.
   - **Assigned:** Dispatched to jurisdictional engineering department and officer.
   - **In Progress:** Field response team active on site.
   - **Resolved:** Remediation completed and certified with photographic evidence.

---

## 5. Providing Feedback & Appeals (கருத்து & மறுஆய்வு)

- **Feedback:** Once a grievance is marked **Resolved**, click **Rate Resolution** on your dashboard or tracker to submit a 1–5 star rating and feedback note.
- **Appeal:** If the issue has not been satisfactorily resolved on the ground, click **Request Appeal / Reopen**, state your reason (min 10 characters), and the complaint will be returned to **Supervisor Review**.

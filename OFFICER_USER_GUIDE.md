# CivicResolve — Field Officer Operations Guide

> **Notice:** This manual guides municipal field engineers, electrical supervisors, drainage teams, and sanitation supervisors managing assigned grievances on the ground.

---

## 1. Officer Portal Access

1. Navigate to the CivicResolve header and select **Officer Portal** (or log in at `/login` using officer credentials).
2. Sample Departmental Accounts:
   - **Greater Chennai Corporation Zonal Officer:** `gcc.officer@civicresolve.gov`
   - **TANGEDCO Electrical Engineer:** `tangedco.officer@civicresolve.gov`
   - **CMWSSB / Water & Drainage Engineer:** `cmwssb.officer@civicresolve.gov`
3. The **Officer Tasks View** displays grievances specifically filtered to your assigned name, department, and district.

---

## 2. Managing Field Work Orders

### Task Cards Overview
Each task card displays:
- **Reference ID & Urgency:** Priority badge (Critical / High / Medium / Low).
- **SLA Countdown Timer:** Real-time remaining hours before SLA breach.
- **Location & Ward:** Interactive map coordinates and street address.
- **Citizen Contact Information:** Phone and name (unmasked for jurisdictional officers).

### Updating Work Progress
1. When arriving on site, click **Update Progress / File Report**.
2. Update status:
   - **Action in Progress:** Work crews actively deployed.
   - **Awaiting Citizen Info:** Further access details required.
   - **Evidence Submitted:** Civil repair complete, awaiting supervisor sign-off.
   - **Resolved:** Remediation fully completed.
3. Enter **Official Remarks** (visible to citizen) explaining the corrective measures taken.
4. Enter **Internal Notes** (confidential to municipal administration).
5. Attach **Photographic Evidence URL** (e.g., photo of asphalted pothole or repaired water pipeline).
6. Click **Save Report**.

### Handling Overdue / Blocked Tasks
If a task cannot be completed within the SLA due to external dependencies (e.g. road excavation permit, heavy rain):
- File an update with status **Under Investigation** or notify the Zonal Officer.
- The complaint's status history retains your explanation to protect officer performance metrics.
